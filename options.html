Privacy boundary

The complete timestamped transcript is sent to a compatible stable Gemini Flash model to choose visually important moments; retrying after a service failure may send it again. Selected lecture frames and nearby transcript context are then sent to the frame-reading model. WiseNotes builds the final universal prompt locally and never sends it to another LLM provider; you choose where to paste it.
